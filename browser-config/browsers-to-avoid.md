Even if you aren't using my recommended browser setup, you should stay away from these browsers.

## Wave Browser

This one is definitely a PUP/PUA. It claims to "save the ocean", but it actually injects ads into your search results. These often go to fake websites, making it easier to get scammed.

_(I probably spent too much time creating this section)_

### Reasons to Avoid

- Has paid subscription-based features
- Injects scam ads into search results (and the company blames it on Yahoo)
- Often bundled with other unwanted software
- Classified as unwanted software by multiple antimalware programs
- Lots of bad reviews on [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wavebrowser.co)

### Counter-Arguments About Its Claims

- **Ad Blocker:** Not only is it nowhere near as good as uBlock Origin (and other free ad blockers), but it's also a paid feature. Also, on [this page](https://wavebrowser.co/features/ad-block), it says "Ditch the extensions, built-in ad blocking does more", and then [another page on the same website](https://wavebrowser.co/integrated-extensions) explains how the ad blocker uses a browser extension.
- **AI Assistant:** This almost certainly tracks you and sends all your conversations to the company. Also, it doesn't do anything special that you can't do in other browsers (where you can just pin ChatGPT to the sidebar).
- **As Seen On <Review Website>:** Click on an article. Most of them are either written by the same person (someone named "Cole Martin"), written by Wave Browser's own brand profile, or sponsored.
- **Multitask with Split View:** You don't need to look very hard to find this feature in almost any other browser.
- **Built-In Productivity Features:** It's just a gimmick. You can easily use browser extensions, external software, or even regular websites to write notes and convert files.
- **Messaging Features:** All it is is social media websites pinned to your sidebar. You can easily do this in other browsers. And if you look closely at the privacy and security section of [this page](https://wavebrowser.co/features/messaging), you'll see that all those features are "provided by the messaging service" and are not exclusive to Wave Browser.
- **Built-In Free PDF Annotator:** Look closely at the screenshots. It's exactly the same as the one used in Firefox.
- **Translator:** This "amazing" feature that they advertise is actually standard in all modern web browsers.
- **Get the best Amazon deals:** With the semi-recent [Honey scandal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vc4yL3YTwWk), I wouldn't trust a feature like this. Also, it uses a third-party website, so it's not exclusive to Wave Browser.

There are a whole bunch of other features that they advertise, even though they're standard in all web browsers.

## AOL Shield (Pro)
Paid subscription for features you don't need or can get for free in other browsers. Also does the same ad injection that Wave Browser does.

## Chromium Netscape
AOL Shield Pro, but without the paid features. It still injects malicious ads into your search results.

## Opera GX
Not quite as bad as Wave or AOL Shield, but it shouldn't be trusted. The "gaming features" are mostly visual, and it's just an excuse to show you ads for video games. The performance features are less efficient than other browsers. The "VPN" is actually a proxy (it doesn't encrypt your traffic) that goes through a Chinese server. It's often bundled with other unwanted software and often comes from drive-by downloads.
